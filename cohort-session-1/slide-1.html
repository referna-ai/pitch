Our Values

  • Give FirstThe flywheel starts with what you offer, not what you're owed.
  • Timing Is EverythingA referral means nothing until someone's actually at their buying moment. Learn to recognize it in the people around you.
  • Real Relationships1:1s aren't small talk — they're how trust, and referrals, actually get built.
  • Honest, Constructive FeedbackThe circle pulls each other up. Real feedback, not polite silence, is how everyone actually gets better.
  • Specific AsksA vague "let me know if you hear of anything" gets nothing. A clear ask gets referred correctly.
Three of these already show up in the product's point system. The other two — timing and feedback — are what we teach because the app can't.
